01 — About the service

A subtle definition for the eyes, without heavy makeup

For lash-line enhancement, pigment is placed along the lash roots to make visible gaps less noticeable. With the eyes open, the result should look subtle and complement their natural shape.

The suitable line thickness, color and length are considered for each eye. Eye and skin conditions are assessed before the procedure.

  • Design suited to your eye shape
  • A natural look without makeup
  • Aftercare guidance
  • Review of relevant conditions and sensitivities

03 — Frequently asked questions

Before lash-line enhancement

How is lash-line enhancement different from permanent eyeliner?

Lash-line enhancement is usually applied very finely along the lash roots; eyeliner can be wider and may have a distinct wing.

Is the result visible when the eyes are closed?

The aim is not to create a heavy line on the eyelid. A suitable design focuses on making the lash roots look fuller.

Should I mention contact lenses or eye sensitivities?

Yes. Please mention any sensitivities, eye treatment, medication or contact lens use before the procedure.
