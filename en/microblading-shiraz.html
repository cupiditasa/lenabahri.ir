01 — About the Service

Brow design that starts with your features

Microblading creates fine, hair-like strokes in sparse areas of the brows. Before the procedure, your face shape, eye spacing, natural hair growth and preferences are considered to guide a balanced, natural-looking design.

This technique is not the same choice for every skin type. During a consultation, we discuss whether microblading, nano brows or a combination may better suit your needs.

  • Brow mapping before the procedure
  • Color selected to complement your features
  • Aftercare instructions

03 — Frequently Asked Questions

Before Microblading

How long does microblading last?

Longevity varies with skin type, lifestyle, aftercare and individual response; there is no fixed duration that applies to everyone.

Is the brow shape decided before the procedure?

Yes. The design and mapping are completed first, and the procedure begins only after you review and approve the shape.

Is it suitable for oily skin?

For oily skin, another technique such as nano brows or shading may provide a more even, longer-lasting result. The final choice is discussed during consultation.
