Selection guide

How can delicate microblading complement your features?

Microblading uses fine strokes inspired by natural hair growth to make sparse areas appear more defined. A delicate result is not only about thin lines: the starting point, arch, spacing and harmony with natural brows matter too.

Brow design should be tailored to each face

Before choosing a shape, we consider eye shape, brow bone, hair density, skin type and personal preference. A reference photo or trend is not a universal prescription; review and approve the proposed design before the procedure.

What should you ask before booking?

Ask during your consultation whether the technique suits your skin, what color to expect, how healing works and what aftercare is needed. Longevity and final appearance vary by individual.

Before you decide

A complimentary consultation is available after an assessment. Technique and color depend on individual circumstances; the same result cannot be guaranteed for everyone.

Frequently asked questions

Is natural-looking microblading right for everyone?

No. Skin condition, hair density and your desired look all affect technique choice. A consultation and assessment beforehand can help identify a more suitable option.
