Selection guide

How is lash-line enhancement different from eyeliner?

Lash-line enhancement focuses on a fine line near the lash roots, while eyeliner can be wider and extend farther outward. Exact thickness and length depend on eye shape and the agreed design.

A subtle design does not mean one fixed shape

Lash spacing, eyelid shape and your desired result all inform the design. Before the procedure, clarify placement and color intensity so your expectations align with the proposal.

Consultation before permanent eye makeup

If you have a history of eye sensitivity, surgery or a skin condition, mention it before booking so suitability can be assessed. This beauty service is not a substitute for medical evaluation.

Before you decide

A complimentary consultation is available after an assessment. Technique and color depend on individual circumstances; the same result cannot be guaranteed for everyone.

Frequently asked questions

Is lash-line enhancement the same as permanent eyeliner?

Not exactly. Lash-line enhancement is usually finer and closer to the lash roots. The final shape depends on the agreed design and individual eye characteristics.
