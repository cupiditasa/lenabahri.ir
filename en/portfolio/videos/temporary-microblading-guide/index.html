Selection guide

“Temporary” does not mean it fades by a set date

With permanent makeup, pigment may fade over time, but residual color, fading speed and the need for correction differ from person to person. “Temporary” is not a guarantee of complete removal or a specific timeline.

What factors affect pigment longevity?

Skin type, pigment choice, technique, lifestyle, sun exposure and aftercare can all affect how color looks over time. Even similar circumstances do not guarantee the same result for two people.

Set clear expectations before deciding

Ask the specialist about healing, possible residual pigment and correction options. If you only want to try a shape, discuss temporary options without pigment; choose a method that matches the level of commitment you are comfortable with.

Before you decide

A complimentary consultation is available after an assessment. Technique and color depend on individual circumstances; the same result cannot be guaranteed for everyone.

Frequently asked questions

Exactly how many months until microblading disappears?

There is no fixed timeline for everyone, and pigment may not disappear completely. Before the procedure, discuss possible residual pigment and correction with a specialist.
