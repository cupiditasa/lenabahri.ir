Selection guide

What does a soft, natural look mean?

This style usually aims for a neat, soft brow—not an exaggerated arch or uniform density from end to end. Stroke design, spacing and pigment intensity can make the result look softer or more defined.

Small details make a difference

Hair-growth direction, sparse areas and the natural shape of both brows guide the design. Instead of following a preset template, discuss how much change you want and which features should remain natural.

An informed choice for your brows

Review the initial design and suggested color before the procedure, and ask about healing and aftercare. The final result depends on skin and individual response; example photos do not guarantee the same result for someone else.

Before you decide

A complimentary consultation is available after an assessment. Technique and color depend on individual circumstances; the same result cannot be guaranteed for everyone.

Frequently asked questions

Does natural hairstroke design mean no visible change?

The degree of change depends on the design and your brows. During consultation, discuss how subtle or defined you want the result so the proposed design can reflect your expectations.
