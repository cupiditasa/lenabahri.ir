Selection guide

How do three common brow shapes differ?

A straight shape usually has a lower arch, a soft arch creates a subtle lift, and a more defined arch draws attention to the upper brow. These are broad descriptions; real designs can fall between them.

Brow shape and facial expression

A small change in brow angle or tail can alter the visual impression of your eyes. Rather than following a popular style, compare examples with your natural shape and discuss the degree of change you want.

Review the design before choosing

During consultation, review the proposed shape on your face and share your preferences for arch, length and density. The final design should be clear and approved before the procedure begins.

Before you decide

A complimentary consultation is available after an assessment. Technique and color depend on individual circumstances; the same result cannot be guaranteed for everyone.

Frequently asked questions

Do arched brows always make a face look better?

No. Beauty depends on overall balance and personal preference. The arch should suit your natural brows and expression rather than a general rule.
