Selection guide

What is the purpose of lip shading?

Lip shading is a beauty service intended to add color and create a more even appearance. Color is selected with your natural lip tone, preferences and desired design in mind; results are not identical for everyone or guaranteed to match a reference photo.

Choose a realistic expectation for the final color

The color immediately after the procedure can differ from the healed appearance. Natural lip tone, healing and skin response affect the final look; ask about these differences before booking.

When is consultation especially important?

If your lips are sensitive, irritated or have an unusual color change, have the condition assessed before considering a beauty service. The suggested design and color should be discussed after an individual assessment.

Before you decide

A complimentary consultation is available after an assessment. Technique and color depend on individual circumstances; the same result cannot be guaranteed for everyone.

Frequently asked questions

Does lip shading guarantee the exact color you want?

No. Natural lip tone and healing affect the result. Examples are general references; final color should be chosen after an individual assessment with realistic expectations.
