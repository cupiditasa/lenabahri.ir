Selection guide

The main difference is the finished look

Some methods focus on hair-like strokes, while others create a more even, shaded effect. Terms such as microblading, hairstrokes and nano brows may be used differently across the industry; a technique name alone does not explain every procedure detail.

Choose a technique for your skin and brow shape

Hair density, sparse areas, skin type and your desired look all influence the choice. Fine strokes, for example, may appear less distinct on some skin types; an in-person assessment is more reliable than choosing from a photo alone.

What should you ask during a consultation?

Ask exactly how the procedure is performed, how the healed result differs from day one, what aftercare is needed and whether another option may suit you better. Comparing techniques against your needs is more useful than choosing a trend name.

Before you decide

A complimentary consultation is available after an assessment. Technique and color depend on individual circumstances; the same result cannot be guaranteed for everyone.

Frequently asked questions

Which brow technique lasts longer?

There is no single answer for everyone. Skin, pigment, procedure method and aftercare affect longevity, so the choice should follow an individual assessment.
