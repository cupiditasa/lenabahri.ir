Selection guide

Clarify exactly what PhiBrows and microblading mean

In everyday use, PhiBrows and microblading may refer to similar styles or different methods. During consultation, do not rely on the label alone; ask specifically about the tools, stroke pattern and expected appearance.

Hairstrokes should complement your natural brows

Hair-growth direction, sparse areas and overall brow density all matter in design. Adding more strokes does not always look more natural; spacing and shape should suit each person's brows.

From decision to an informed procedure

Before proceeding, ask whether the method suits your skin, what healing involves and what aftercare is needed. If you are deciding between techniques, comparing designs and discussing them with a specialist can help.

Before you decide

A complimentary consultation is available after an assessment. Technique and color depend on individual circumstances; the same result cannot be guaranteed for everyone.

Frequently asked questions

Are PhiBrows and microblading always the same method?

Not necessarily. Providers may use these names differently. Before booking, ask about the exact tools, method and expected result.
