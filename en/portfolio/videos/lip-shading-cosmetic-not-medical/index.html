Selection guide

What does lip shading do?

Lip shading is intended to create a makeup-like color effect. It is a beauty procedure, not a medical diagnosis or treatment, and should not be used to address disease, sores, inflammation or the cause of unusual lip-color changes.

Cosmetic goals versus medical treatment

If lip discoloration is sudden, persistent, painful or accompanied by sores, seek medical advice before any cosmetic procedure. Beauty services should be considered only for healthy lips and after assessing individual circumstances.

Choose color with realistic expectations

Natural lip tone, healing and aftercare affect the final appearance; a guaranteed or identical result cannot be promised for everyone. Ask about limitations, color design and recovery during consultation.

Before you decide

A complimentary consultation is available after an assessment. Technique and color depend on individual circumstances; the same result cannot be guaranteed for everyone.

Frequently asked questions

Does lip shading treat dark lips or lip conditions?

No. Lip shading is a cosmetic service, not medical treatment. If you have discoloration, pain or sores, consult a medical professional first.
