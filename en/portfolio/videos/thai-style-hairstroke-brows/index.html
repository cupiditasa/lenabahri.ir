Selection guide

What does Thai style mean in hairstroke brows?

The name describes an aesthetic approach to brow design, often recognized by directional strokes and a defined shape. Artists may use style labels differently, so review examples and the proposed design carefully before the procedure.

How can the shape be balanced with your features?

The brow arch, space between the brows, hair-growth direction and stroke intensity all affect the overall look. A trending style may not suit every face; personalized design is a more considered choice.

Consultation before hairstroke brows

Discuss the technique, color, shape and realistic expectations during consultation. An assessment of your skin and brows matters more than relying only on a style name or reference photo.

Before you decide

A complimentary consultation is available after an assessment. Technique and color depend on individual circumstances; the same result cannot be guaranteed for everyone.

Frequently asked questions

Does Thai style suit every face shape?

Not necessarily. A style name alone does not determine suitability. The design should complement facial structure, brow growth and personal preference.
