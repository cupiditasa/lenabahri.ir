Selection guide

Face shape is not the only design factor

Face-shape guides can start a conversation, but good design is not limited to labels such as round or long. Eye spacing, brow bone, facial expression and natural hair growth should also be considered.

Preserve your facial character in brow design

Raising the arch or changing brow length can alter the expression of your eyes. The degree of change should reflect your wishes, and the initial design should be reviewed before the procedure; the aim is not to impose a standard shape.

How can you prepare for consultation?

Bring examples of shapes you like and explain what you do not want changed. During an in-person assessment, the suggested shape and technique can be considered in light of your brows.

Before you decide

A complimentary consultation is available after an assessment. Technique and color depend on individual circumstances; the same result cannot be guaranteed for everyone.

Frequently asked questions

Is there one specific brow shape for every face shape?

No. Face-shape categories are only general guides. Final design should suit your actual features, brow growth and preferences.
