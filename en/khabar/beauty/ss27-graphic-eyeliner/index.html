Colorful Graphic Eyeliner: Small Detail, Big Impact

A single colored line can anchor a makeup look; there is no need to cover the entire lid.

Written by: Lena Bahri Beauty Journal Editorial TeamReviewed by: Lena BahriOctober 10, 20264 min read

A colored line around the eyes can be a simple way to add color to makeup. Runway reports offer visual inspiration, but shape and intensity can be adapted for personal style and everyday wear.

Eye details in fashion-week coverage

Who What Wear’s October 2026 report reviews several looks from Spring/Summer 2027 shows. The selected images are examples of fashion design, not proof of a settled trend or that the makeup suits everyone.

Keep color precise and restrained

Try a fine line near the lashes or outer corner with an eye pencil; sketch lightly first for symmetry. Do not use unapproved products or products made for lips or the body around the eyes.
