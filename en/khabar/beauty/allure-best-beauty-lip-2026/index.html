2026 Beauty Awards: How to Assess the Lip Winners for Yourself

A media award can help you discover a product, but it does not guarantee the same result or constitute official endorsement for every consumer.

Written by: Lena Bahri Beauty Journal Editorial TeamReviewed by: Lena BahriOctober 10, 20264 min read

Annual beauty awards publish selected products across categories. An editorial choice is not medical advice, a guarantee that a product will work for everyone, or a full review of availability and prices in Iran.

What does a publication’s winners list mean?

On September 17, 2026, Allure announced its lip-product winners in the Best of Beauty collection. The list reflects that publication’s selection process and market; prices, availability and product versions can differ elsewhere.

Three questions to ask before buying

Consider whether you like the texture, whether the ingredients suit you and whether an authentic product is available. When comparing options, separate marketing claims and personal experience from the publication’s judging criteria.
