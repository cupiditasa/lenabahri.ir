Permanent Makeup Pigments and the FDA: U.S. Guidance Is Not Iranian Regulation

FDA guidance applies to the United States; this page is not a substitute for Iranian regulations or professional advice.

Written by: Lena Bahri Beauty Journal Editorial TeamReviewed by: Lena BahriOctober 10, 20264 min read

Permanent makeup places pigment into the skin, making questions about ingredients and hygiene important. FDA information describes the U.S. framework; it should not be presented as Iranian law or as a safety guarantee for a particular brand or studio.

What does the FDA page say?

The FDA’s official page on tattoos and permanent makeup discusses, among other topics, the status of color additives injected into the skin and some reported risks. This guidance concerns U.S. oversight and does not state Iranian law.

Useful questions before choosing a studio

Ask about pigment ingredients and labeling, sealed sterile packs, single-use cartridges, studio hygiene and aftercare. If you have allergies or a skin condition, consult a clinician; check local rules and licensing with the responsible Iranian authorities.
