Beauty Journal Editorial Policy

Transparency about the authorship, sources, imagery and limitations of this collection.

Authorship and Specialist Review

Articles in this collection are published under the byline “Lena Bahri Beauty Journal Editorial Team,” the website’s editorial name. Lena Bahri, master artist and permanent makeup educator, has read and approved every article. This review does not mean she authored the articles or medically endorsed their content.

How We Prepare Articles

AI tools assist with Persian drafts and summaries, and each report links to its source on the same page. These articles are editorial summaries and explanations of public sources. They do not claim independent product testing, exclusive interviews or treatment experience unless the page explicitly documents it.

Sources and Geographic Scope

The source date is listed separately from the Persian publication date. Information from foreign agencies such as the FDA applies to their jurisdictions and is not an interpretation of Iranian law.

Images and Health Topics

Images labeled as AI illustrations are not photographs of real events, products, workplaces or client results. Safety and skincare articles are not medical advice and do not replace consultation with a physician or qualified professional.

Corrections and Feedback

If you find an error or a new source, please contact us through the About Lena and Contact page so we can review the article and correct it if needed.
