Peptides in Skincare: What Is the Evidence for Topical Products?

Research on peptides is promising, but evidence for topical products remains limited.

Written by: Lena Bahri Beauty Journal Editorial TeamReviewed by: Lena BahriOctober 10, 20264 min read

Peptide is a group of compounds, not one formula. A 2026 systematic review examined 19 trials, but 17 studied oral peptides and only two studied topical forms; the overall findings should not be applied to every serum or cream.

What did the evidence review cover?

On January 5, 2026, Vogue mentioned peptides among skincare trends; that source is trend reporting. A systematic review and meta-analysis published in Frontiers in Medicine on March 17, 2026 included 19 trials with 1,341 participants: 17 examined oral peptides and only two examined topical forms. The authors cited formula variability and this imbalance as limits to definitive conclusions.

What should you consider when choosing a product?

The word peptide alone does not tell you the compound, concentration, stability or delivery system. Check the product label and claims, and do not expect every formula to have the same effects. A research review is not an independent test of a specific product or medical advice; stop using a product if irritation occurs and seek professional guidance.
