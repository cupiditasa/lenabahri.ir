A Modern Take on 2000s Frosted Makeup: Gloss and Shimmer in Balance

A modern version borrows one detail—such as glossy lips or a light shimmer—instead of copying the entire look.

Written by: Lena Bahri Beauty Journal Editorial TeamReviewed by: Lena BahriOctober 10, 20264 min read

Beauty nostalgia offers room for reinterpretation: glossy lips, light shimmer and playful color. A modern look does not require every element at once; one detail can be enough.

Fashion inspiration, not an exact recreation

Who What Wear’s guide, published January 29, 2026, forecast a return of some retro beauty cues. This article’s take on Y2K nostalgia is an editorial suggestion, not a claim that the look is widespread or suits everyone.

Choose one glossy element

Make lip gloss or a light eyeshadow the focal point, and choose a texture that feels comfortable. Lip and eye products have different intended uses; do not use one in place of the other.
