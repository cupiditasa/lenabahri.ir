Pink Brows on 2027 Runways: Try the Playful Trend Without Commitment

Colorful brows are an editorial choice; tinted gel and temporary makeup are lower-commitment alternatives to a lasting change.

Written by: Lena Bahri Beauty Journal Editorial TeamReviewed by: Lena BahriOctober 10, 20264 min read

Colorful brows stand out on the runway, turning a facial feature into a fashion statement. But these looks are inspiration, not everyday instructions; a temporary, removable option is a more practical way to try the color.

A runway trend is different from an everyday choice

Who What Wear’s Spring/Summer 2027 coverage collects makeup and styling seen on the runway. These looks do not prove a lasting trend or demand in Iran’s market.

Keep color temporary and eye-safe

To try the look, use tinted brow gel or cosmetic shadow in your preferred shade and remove it before bed. Keep hair dye and products not intended for the eye area away from your eyes; stop use if irritation occurs.
