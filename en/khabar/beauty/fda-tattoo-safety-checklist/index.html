Permanent Makeup Hygiene Checklist: Questions to Ask Before You Begin

Seeing sealed sterile supplies and a single-use cartridge, and discussing aftercare, can make a consultation clearer.

Written by: Lena Bahri Beauty Journal Editorial TeamReviewed by: Lena BahriOctober 10, 20264 min read

Quality is not only about appearance; informed consent, hygiene and aftercare matter too. FDA guidance can prompt useful questions, but local legal standards and professional licensing should be checked with Iranian authorities.

General U.S. Guidance

On October 24, 2024, the FDA issued final guidance on unsanitary conditions and microbial contamination risks in tattoo ink manufacturing and distribution. It applies to manufacturers and distributors in the United States; the hygiene questions here are general prompts for discussion.

Questions for an open conversation

Before starting, ask about materials, single-use cartridges, opening sealed supplies in front of the client, disposal and aftercare. If answers are unclear or the setting does not appear hygienic, postpone your decision.
