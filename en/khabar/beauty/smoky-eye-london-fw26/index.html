Gray Smoky Eyes at London Fashion Week: Soft Texture, Not Harsh Lines

Runway smoky gray eyes use softly blended edges; the intensity can be adjusted for your features and occasion.

Written by: Lena Bahri Beauty Journal Editorial TeamReviewed by: Lena BahriOctober 10, 20264 min read

Smoky eyes have returned to fashion-week coverage, but a modern version need not be dark or heavy. Vogue’s London coverage presents diffused color and soft texture as one way to reinterpret this classic.

What the London report captured

Vogue’s February 27, 2026 report reviews looks seen at London Fashion Week for Fall/Winter 2026. Its selections are editorial and should not be treated as representative of every show or audience preference.

How can you soften the blended look?

Instead of drawing a bold line, apply a neutral shade in light layers near the lashes and blend the edges softly. Use products made for the eye area if your eyes are sensitive, and do not share makeup tools.
