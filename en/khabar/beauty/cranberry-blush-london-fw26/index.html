Cranberry Blush: A Cool Runway Shade for Everyday Makeup

Berry blush can range from a soft flush to a dramatic statement; undertone and amount of product matter.

Written by: Lena Bahri Beauty Journal Editorial TeamReviewed by: Lena BahriOctober 10, 20264 min read

Berry and cranberry tones are associated with cooler seasons, but a deep application is not needed for every occasion. Vogue’s London Fashion Week report documents expressive makeup looks; here, we translate the idea into practical guidance on controlling color intensity.

How does color feature in the report?

Vogue’s February 27, 2026 report on London Fashion Week beauty trends discusses cheek and eye color within fall/winter styling. Its image selection covers the shows featured in the report and is not a measure of sales.

Start with a soft flush

Apply a small amount of blush to the apples of the cheeks and blend the edges; then check the intensity in natural light. Permanent lip or face color has a different process and lasting result from temporary makeup, and should be discussed separately.
