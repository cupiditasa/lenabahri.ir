Skincare Actives in 2026: Separate Trends from Product Evidence

Treat trend reports as a starting point for questions; assess each product’s formula, evidence and directions separately.

Written by: Lena Bahri Beauty Journal Editorial TeamReviewed by: Lena BahriOctober 10, 20264 min read

Annual forecasts bring new ingredient names into beauty conversations. But the words exosome, peptide or any other ingredient alone do not establish the clinical effectiveness of a finished product; evaluate each product’s formula, claims and evidence separately.

What does the trend report say?

Who What Wear’s guide, published January 29, 2026, reviews emerging beauty topics, including active ingredients and skin biotechnology. It is a media forecast, not a clinical trial or health-agency approval of a specific product.

An ingredient name is not enough

Check the full ingredient list, directions and manufacturer’s warnings; do not start a new product alongside several other actives at once. If you have a skin condition, allergies or a medical concern, consult a dermatologist before changing your routine.
