Silver Eyeliner: A Metallic Accent for Party Makeup

A metallic accent can be as subtle as a fine line; use only products made for the eye area.

Written by: Lena Bahri Beauty Journal Editorial TeamReviewed by: Lena BahriOctober 10, 20264 min read

Metallic color can add a party-ready accent to simple makeup. Runway inspiration does not make every pigment suitable for the eye area; check the product label and remove makeup properly.

From the Runway to Evening Makeup

Who What Wear’s Spring/Summer 2027 beauty report documents a range of eye details and colors. This article uses it as fashion context; the accompanying image is an illustration created for this story.

Choose a product made for the eye area

Choose shadow or a pencil labeled for eye use and keep it close to the lash line. If you wear contact lenses or have sensitive eyes, follow the manufacturer’s directions and remove the makeup if irritation occurs.
