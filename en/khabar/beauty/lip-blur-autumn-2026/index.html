Fall 2026 Lip Makeup: Blurred Edges and Soft Color

Makeup artists’ fall 2026 forecasts focus on soft, blurred lips, lightweight textures and buildable color.

Written by: Lena Bahri Beauty Journal Editorial TeamReviewed by: Lena BahriOctober 10, 20264 min read

Makeup artist forecasts for fall 2026 move away from sharp lines and heavy coverage, favoring blurred edges, buildable color and more comfortable everyday textures.

What does the source say about fall 2026 lip makeup?

A September 15, 2026 Who What Wear report, citing makeup artist Annabella Brayne, highlights six directions—from blurred and “bitten” lips to glossy, balm-like textures and 1990s browns.

A shared theme is softer color edges and adjustable intensity. These are seasonal makeup ideas, not one formula for every face.

Blurred makeup is not the same as permanent shading

The report’s blurred lip look is created with removable makeup. Lip shading places pigment in the skin, and its result and longevity depend on the individual, technique and aftercare; a temporary trend cannot be directly turned into a permanent shape or color.

If you like a fall shade, try it first with lipstick or tint. For a shading decision, discuss natural lip color, skin undertone and the healed result in a separate consultation.

What are the limits of this report?

This is a media forecast for an international audience; it does not provide Iranian search data or local sales figures. Seasonal trends may not match every client’s preferences or features.
