01 — Learning Path

From technique practice to professional decision-making

The goal is not to memorize one fixed pattern. Students learn to consider skin type, facial features, client needs and the limitations of each technique in order to make informed choices.

Course level and content depend on the student’s prior experience. Schedule, syllabus, availability and support details are provided before enrollment.
