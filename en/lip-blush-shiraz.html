01 — About the Service

A fresher lip tone, without a heavy lipstick effect

Lip shading aims to balance color, subtly refine unevenness and define the natural lip shape. Once healed, the color should look soft and harmonious with your features.

The shade is selected based on your natural lip color, skin tone and preferences. Skin condition and any history of cold sores should be discussed before the procedure.

03 — Frequently Asked Questions

Before Lip Shading

When is the healed color visible?

As the initial intensity settles and the skin heals, the shade usually appears softer and closer to its healed result.

Is lip shading the same as permanent lipstick?

The goal is generally a natural-looking tint and subtle shape refinement—not a full, heavy lipstick effect.

Does a history of cold sores matter?

Yes. Please disclose relevant medical history before booking so appropriate guidance can be provided, including referral to a healthcare professional if needed.
