03 — Frequently Asked Questions

Nano Brows or Shading?

What is the difference between nano brows and brow shading?

Nano brows create hair-like strokes; shading gives a softer, more uniform, powder-like finish. The choice depends on your skin and desired look.

Can the two techniques be combined?

Yes. For some brows, fine strokes near the front combined with light shading in sparse areas can create a more balanced look.

Will the brow color look too dark?

The initial color often looks more intense and softens as the skin heals. The shade is selected to suit your features.
